    • Venus

      The second planet from the sun and is the hottest planet in the solar system. Its thick atmosphere is extremely toxic and composed sulfuric acid clouds,the planet is an example of the greenhouse effect.Venus spins slowly from east to west,the ooposite direction of most of the other planets.
    • Uranus

      It has clouds made of hydrogen sulfide, the same chemical that makes rotten eggs smell so foul. It rotates from east to west like Venus..
    • Mars

      The fourth planet from the sun.It is a cold,desert-like planet covered in iron oxide dust that gives the planet its signature red hue.Mars has similarities like Earth like valleys,canyons and storm systems.Substantial scientific evidence suggests that Mars was a warmer,wetter world and that conditions Mars would have had the conditionns to support life like bactria and other microbes. .

    • Jupiter

      The fifth planet from the sun and the largest planet in the solar system. The gas giant is more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined.Its swirling clouds are colorful due to different types of trace gases including ammonia ice,ammonium hydrosulfide crystals as well as ice and vapor.

    • Saturn

      The sixth planet from the sun and is famous for its large and distinct ring system.The gaseous planet is mostly composed of hydrogen and helium.If you put Saturn in a bathtub it would float as Saturn has an average density that is less than water.

    • Uranus

      The seventh planet from the sun and is a bit of an oddball.It has clouds made of hydrogen sulfide,the same chemical that makes rotten eggs smell so foul.It rotates from east to west and its equator is nearly at right angles to its orbit.

    • Neptune

      The eighth planet from the sun and is on average the coldest planet in the solar system.The average is at the top of the clouds which is 346F(minus 210 degrees Celcius)Neptune is approximately the size as Uranus and is known for its supersonic strong winds.The planet is more than 30 times away from the sun and it was the first planetbto be predicted to exist by using maths,rather than being visually detected.
